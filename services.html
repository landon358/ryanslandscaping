Services

Everything your property needs, from one local crew.

Most of our customers started with one service and ended up handing us the whole property. That's the idea — you make one call, and the mowing, the beds, the patio, the trees and the snow all get handled by people who already know your place.

Core service · Summer

Lawn & Property Maintenance

Weekly mowing with lines you'll notice, string trimming, bed edging, mulch supply and install, and spring and fall cleanups with dedicated leaf boxes.

Core service · Winter

Snow Removal

Plowing and snow removal that keeps your drive open all winter — plus holiday light installation.

Landscape Design & Installation

Trees, shrubs, layered foundation beds, sod, seeding and seasonal colour — Japanese maples, arborvitae, hydrangea and boxwood are our regulars.

Hardscaping & Stonework

Paver, travertine and flagstone patios, walkways, fire pits, outdoor fireplaces, retaining and boulder walls, stone steps, and shoreline boulder stabilisation.

Tree Services

Trimming, shaping, large removals and storm or ice-damage cleanup, handled by our own crew and equipment.

Our story

It started with one lawn and a push mower.

In 2000, Ryan Fiel was eighteen years old, mowing a single lawn to help pay for college. Twenty-six years later, Ryan's Landscaping runs three crews out of a shop on Deer Lake Road, employs about twenty people, and takes care of properties from downtown Boyne City to the far side of Lake Charlevoix.

What hasn't changed is what the business was built on: showing up when we said we would, doing the job properly, and keeping the same customers for decades.
